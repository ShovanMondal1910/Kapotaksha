Clean School - No Mosquito

On 13 April 2026, Kapotaksha High School (KHS) successfully organized the Clean School - No Mosquito on campus. The grand celebration honoured Goddess Durga and brought the community together in a vibrant expression of devotion and cultural heritage.

Event Highlights

  • A campaign to maintain the cleanliness of the school
  • A campaign to prevent mosquito infestation
  • Cleaning the school premises
  • Awareness programs

KHS is committed to maintaining a clean and healthy environment for all students and staff. The Clean School - No Mosquito campaign is part of our ongoing efforts to promote hygiene, sanitation, and well-being on campus. students, faculty, and the wider community in a shared spirit of devotion and festivity.
