Student Life & Leadership

Clubs & Co-Curricular Activities

Nurturing leadership, creativity, sportsmanship, and civic duty beyond the classroom. At Kapotaksha High School, every student is encouraged to discover their passions and cultivate lifelong skills.

🏛️ Active Clubs: 8 Societies
👥 Student Members: 350+ Students
🏆 Annual Events: 15+ Competitions
🎖️ Awards Won: 25+ Trophies
Explore Societies

Our Student Clubs

Choose from our diverse co-curricular societies guided by dedicated faculty moderators.

Public Speaking

KHS Debating Society

Fosters eloquence, logical reasoning, and argumentation skills in both Bengali and English parliamentary debate formats. Prepares teams for inter-school, upazila, and national championships.

  • 👨‍🏫 Moderator: Senior Language Faculty
  • ⏰ Sessions: Every Tuesday, 3:30 PM
  • 🎯 Key Event: Annual Inter-House Debate Trophy
👥 45 Members
STEM & Robotics

Science & STEM Club

Hands-on scientific inquiry, robotics experiments, coding challenges, math olympiad training, and innovation projects for district and national science fairs.

  • 👨‍🏫 Moderator: Science & ICT Department
  • ⏰ Sessions: Every Wednesday, 3:30 PM
  • 🎯 Key Event: District Inter-School Science Fair
👥 60 Members
Music & Drama

Cultural & Performing Arts

Preserves and celebrates our rich Bengali cultural heritage through vocal music, classical and folk dance, theater productions, poetry recitation, and festival performances.

  • 👨‍🏫 Moderator: Cultural Activities Committee
  • ⏰ Sessions: Every Thursday, 3:00 PM
  • 🎯 Key Event: Puja & Pahela Baishakh Galas
👥 75 Members
Athletics

Sports & Athletics Club

Organizes inter-class football tournaments, cricket leagues, track and field training, badminton championships, and tactical chess competitions to build discipline and fitness.

  • 👨‍🏫 Moderator: Physical Education Department
  • ⏰ Sessions: Mon & Fri, 4:00 PM
  • 🎯 Key Event: Annual Sports Meet & Tournament
👥 90 Members
Scouts & Guides

Scout & Girl Guide Troop

Instills self-discipline, civic duty, outdoor survival, first aid, emergency disaster response, and leadership through national scout jamborees and community camps.

  • 👨‍🏫 Moderator: Certified Scout Leader
  • ⏰ Sessions: Every Saturday, 10:00 AM
  • 🎯 Key Event: Regional Camporee & Rallies
👥 50 Scouts
Creative Writing

Literary & Publications Club

Encourages student writers and poets to express original thoughts. Curates and publishes the termly school wall magazine "Kapotaksha Prabaha" for 21st February and Victory Day.

  • 👨‍🏫 Moderator: Bengali Literature Faculty
  • ⏰ Sessions: Alternate Thursdays, 3:30 PM
  • 🎯 Key Event: Wall Magazine Exhibition
👥 35 Members
Environment

Eco & Coastal Green Club

Promotes coastal climate resilience and mangrove conservation around the Sundarbans region. Leads tree plantation weeks, plastic-free campus campaigns, and clean water drives.

  • 👨‍🏫 Moderator: Geography & Social Science Faculty
  • ⏰ Sessions: Bi-weekly Saturdays, 2:00 PM
  • 🎯 Key Event: Annual Tree Planting Week
👥 40 Members
Visual Arts

Fine Arts & Photography Club

Develops artistic talent in sketching, watercolor painting, calligraphy, cartooning, and digital event photography. Decorates campus stages for all national and religious festivals.

  • 👨‍🏫 Moderator: Art & Design Teacher
  • ⏰ Sessions: Every Friday, 3:00 PM
  • 🎯 Key Event: Annual Fine Arts Exhibition
👥 30 Members
