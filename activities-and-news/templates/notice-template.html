Special Instructions:

  1. All subject examinations will be conducted for the full allocated duration and full marks.
  2. The Multiple Choice Question (MCQ) examination will take place first, immediately followed by the Creative/Theoretical examination without any break in between.
  3. Examinees are strictly prohibited from using or bringing any electronic devices other than an analog wristwatch into the examination hall.
  4. Only examinees of Class 8 and Class 9 are permitted to use Education Board-approved calculators during the examination.
  5. Examinees must carefully review the exam timetable and seat plan before sitting in their designated seats.
  6. Students must attend school in full uniform and enter the examination hall at least 30 minutes before the exam begins.
